From the founder

Why I built this

In September I passed two AI certifications in three days: the Google Cloud Generative AI Leader and the NVIDIA-Certified Professional: Agentic AI — an exam built for people who work with agentic AI systems every day.

I'm a physician. I had no formal training or education in AI or coding. Everything I know, I taught myself — and I had never worked as an AI engineer.

So I used AI to teach me. Scenario-based practice questions, over and over. Memory tricks for the things that wouldn't stick. Quick review sessions through the day. And above all, going back to the concepts I kept getting wrong until they clicked, instead of rereading what I already knew.

It worked, twice. So I built it into an app — first for my son, a college freshman, and now for you. RapidRecall is that method, for your class or your exam.

— Dan Francis, founder

FAQ

Questions people ask

What does RapidRecall AI cost?

It's free to download. Students can try one college course free for a week, and certification learners get 50 free practice questions. Premium, for your classes, is $12.99 a month or $79.99 a year. Exam Pass, for a certification exam, is a single $29.99 payment for 60 days of access and never renews.

What can I upload?

Photos of your notes, a whiteboard or a textbook page. Files such as PDFs, Word documents, slides or a syllabus, from your iPhone, iCloud Drive or OneDrive. A link to one public web page. Or record a lecture right in the app and tap Transcribe.

You can edit the extracted text before it's saved, and duplicates are skipped. Only add material you have the right to use, and check your school's rules before recording a lecture.

Is my study material private?

The app asks for your permission before anything is sent. Your study material goes to Anthropic (Claude) to write your questions and coaching. Lecture audio stays on your iPhone; when you tap Transcribe, it's sent to OpenAI's speech-to-text service and the text comes back. Neither company uses this content to train its models.

Photos and recordings aren't stored on our servers. There are no ads, no advertising trackers, and no analytics in the app. The privacy policy has the full details.

How do I cancel?

Premium is billed by Apple, so you cancel it on your iPhone:

  1. Open Settings and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then RapidRecall AI.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.

You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for. Exam Pass is a one-time purchase, so there's nothing to cancel.
